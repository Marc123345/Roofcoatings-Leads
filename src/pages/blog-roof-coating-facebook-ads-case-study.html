A commercial roof coating contractor came to us with a problem every contractor knows: inconsistent lead flow. Some months were packed. Others were dead. Referrals kept the business alive but couldn't scale it. The contractor had tried a general marketing agency before — spent $4,000 a month for three months and got a handful of leads that wanted full replacements, not coatings.

We built a Facebook Ads campaign from scratch using the same system we run for roof coating contractors across the US. Here's exactly what we did, why it worked, and how you can apply the same framework to your business.

The Challenge

The contractor's situation before we started:

  1. Lead flow was inconsistent — booked one month, empty the next
  2. Low-intent inquiries from people wanting full roof replacements
  3. Service area targeting was too broad — ads reaching people 60+ miles away
  4. No compelling offer — just 'We do roof coatings'
  5. Follow-up was slow — leads sat for 24-48 hours before a callback

The Goal

We set four clear objectives:

  • Generate more qualified local leads — not volume, quality
  • Increase booked inspections and estimates
  • Improve lead quality with niche-specific pre-qualification
  • Create a repeatable Facebook Ads system that scales

The Strategy

We focused exclusively on Facebook Ads — no Google, no HomeAdvisor, no shared leads. The entire campaign was built around five principles:

Narrow targeting to the contractor's actual service radius. A specific offer — free roof coating inspection — instead of generic service promotion. Real project visuals from the contractor's own job sites. Lead Ads with pre-filled forms to reduce friction. And fast follow-up through our AI chatbot that books appointments automatically.

The Offer That Got Attention

The hook was simple: “Free Roof Coating Inspection for Local Property Owners.” This worked because it was specific, low-risk, and tied to a real problem. We didn't say “We do roof coatings.” We said “Find out whether your roof can be restored instead of replaced — for free.”

Limited availability messaging (“Only 5 inspection slots left this week”) added urgency without feeling pushy.

Why the Campaign Worked

  • Ads were hyper-relevant to the local audience
  • The contractor gave people a clear reason to respond
  • The conversion path was frictionless (Lead Ads, not landing pages)
  • Messaging addressed real roofing pain points, not generic services
  • The AI chatbot turned leads into booked appointments in minutes, not days

What Other Contractors Can Learn

The framework is repeatable:

  1. Do not target everyone — narrow to your real service area
  2. Do not run vague offers — give people a specific reason to act
  3. Use real project visuals, not stock photography
  4. Make conversion easy — Lead Ads beat landing pages for most contractors
  5. Measure success by booked jobs, not cheap leads

FAQ

Can Facebook Ads generate commercial roofing leads?

Yes. By narrowing targeting to specific service areas and using offers like free inspections, Facebook Ads consistently produce pre-qualified commercial roofing leads at a lower cost than shared lead platforms.

What results can a roof coating contractor expect?

With an optimized campaign, contractors typically see leads within 48 hours, close rates of 20-35%, and a cost per lead of $30-$60 — roughly 40% less than HomeAdvisor.

What offer works best for roof coating Facebook Ads?

Free roof coating inspections and no-obligation estimates consistently outperform generic service ads. The offer needs to give the prospect a clear, low-risk reason to act now.
